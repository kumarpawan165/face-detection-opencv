How it works

  1. Capture: a frame is taken from the webcam (video is just many photos per second).
  2. Preprocess: the frame is converted to grayscale. It is faster, and face detection does not need colour.
  3. Detect: a pre-trained Haar Cascade classifier slides a window across the image at several scales (detectMultiScale).
  4. Draw: a bounding box is drawn around every face found.

Limitations

  • Can miss tilted or side-facing faces
  • Struggles in low light or strong shadows
  • Can give false positives at low minNeighbors
